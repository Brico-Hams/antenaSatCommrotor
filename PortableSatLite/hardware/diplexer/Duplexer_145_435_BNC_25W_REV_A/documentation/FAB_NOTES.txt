FABRICATION NOTES
- 2-layer FR-4, 1.6 mm
- 2 oz copper recommended
- ENIG preferred
- No soldermask over RF connector center pads
- Use metal enclosure; solder/fasten ground plane to enclosure walls at several points
- Verify BNC footprint against TE 5415025-1 drawing before fabrication
- Keep air-core inductors perpendicular to each other where practical
- Tune with VNA at low power before applying 25 W
