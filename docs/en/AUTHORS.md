## Outreach organization

[Bricohams](https://bricohams.org) is referenced in this project as a
scientific outreach organization for children and adolescents, according
to the identification supplied by the team.

The station can serve as an educational resource about radio, orbits,
mechanics, electronics, and programming. That purpose does not make the
prototype a toy or authorize minors to build, power, or transmit with it
without supervision.

Current authorship does not claim ownership of the models, publications,
libraries, or equipment originally cited. Earlier contributions and sources
are distinguished in [ACKNOWLEDGEMENTS.md](ACKNOWLEDGEMENTS.md) and
[SOURCES.md](SOURCES.md).
