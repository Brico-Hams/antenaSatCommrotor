HELP · Digital receiver (SSTV / APRS)
BricoHams · Anatol EA5KAO · Diego EA5JEF · Pedro Roca Molina

Decodes audio received through the phone's microphone/audio input. It does not transmit.
1. Connect the receiver's audio output to the phone's audio input with an isolated cable (or use the microphone acoustically). Grant the microphone permission.
2. Choose the mode (SSTV or APRS/AX.25 1200 baud) and the audio input.
3. Enter your callsign if you want APRS messaging (stored on the phone only).
4. Start reception. SSTV images and the packet log can be saved.
5. Check the audio level: too high distorts, too low does not decode.
Problems: no image -> check volume and mode; packet errors -> reduce noise and adjust level.
