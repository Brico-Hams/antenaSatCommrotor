HELP · PortableSatStation (BricoHams)
Authors: Anatol EA5KAO · Diego EA5JEF · Pedro Roca Molina — bricohams.org

NOTICE: prototype not validated in the field. Always use a physical emergency stop, protected power and checked limits. Supervised educational activity. The app does not control CAT or PTT.

1. GETTING STARTED
 a) Power the rotor and check that the physical stop works.
 b) Tap "Scan rotors (15 s)". Android 12+ asks for nearby-devices permission; Android 8-11 asks for location only to scan BLE (the app neither uses nor uploads your position).
 c) Explicitly select your rotor and confirm the passkey configured on the device (pairing).
 d) Enter the local API token and tap "Save encrypted token". The token never leaves the phone.
 e) Connect. "Authenticated and versions verified" means the link is valid; it does NOT enable motors.

2. STATUS AND HOMING
 - "Read status" shows mode, UTC time, site and faults.
 - "Physical homing AZ → EL" sets mechanical AZ = -180° and EL = 0°; it is not true north.
 - "STOP motors" always stops. It does not replace the physical stop.

3. CALIBRATION (4 phases)
 1 Clear space, limits and stop. Homing.  2 Level the base and verify EL = 0.  3 Measure the antenna's CURRENT true heading (compass corrected for declination, away from metal; a stationary GNSS does not measure heading).  4 Enter the heading and confirm. After a rehome or power cycle you must calibrate again.

4. DOWNLOADING SATELLITES (TLE)
 - Pick a provider (CelesTrak, AMSAT or PE0SAT) and tap "Download TLEs from selected provider". HTTPS only; no token, location or analytics. The provider sees your IP.
 - If it fails: check Internet; CelesTrak limits repeated downloads (wait a few hours); try another provider (one provider may be down or blocked on your network while another works); or use "Import local TLE file".
 - The last valid cache is kept if a download fails.
 - With more than 100 satellites you must choose a subset (max. 100).

5. SYNCING WITH THE RASPBERRY / CONTROLLER
 - Needs a connected, authenticated rotor, valid UTC time, mode READY or DISARMED, and fresh TLEs.
 - Tap "Transfer whole catalogue over BLE". The controller's catalogue is replaced. Progress is shown in bytes; if nothing advances for 60 s the transfer is cancelled and the link dropped.
 - If it fails: move the phone closer, reconnect, check the rotor's UTC time, reduce the catalogue and retry. Android's transient error 133 is retried automatically twice.

6. PASSES AND TRACKING
 Choose a satellite from the controller's catalogue and query passes. SGP4 is computed by the controller, not the phone.

7. DIGITAL RECEIVER and RADIO
 The "Digital receiver" and "Radio host" buttons have their own help (Help button on each screen).

8. COMMON PROBLEMS
 - Rotor not found: Bluetooth on, permission granted, rotor advertising, within 5 m.
 - "bond_and_token_required": pair in Android settings and save the token.
 - "authentication_failed": wrong token.
 - "protocol_mismatch": different app/firmware versions.
 - "ble_request_timeout": weak signal; reconnect.
